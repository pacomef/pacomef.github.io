Quick summary:
- Mathraining is the site that introduced me to olympiad-style math.
- I'm now ranked in the top 350 of the site, out of a bit more than 25,000 ranked users. That means solving, with a proof to submit, a lot of exercises, including exercises from the IMO and other prestigious competitions.
- I've also been a grader for a few months now, and I very humbly help reduce the workload for the other graders.
</div>

Mathraining is a training platform for olympiad-style mathematics. It originally started as a Belgian site, but very quickly became a reference in the French community as well.

I discovered Mathraining at the end of high school, aiming to prepare for the "concours général" math competition (and I'm honestly a bit sad I discovered it so late). I spent a few days on it, then came back during my summer break, to prepare for the classes préparatoires starting the following year. That's when I really got into olympiad math, even though I'd already dabbled a bit through Project Euler, but it was also the first French site where I could interact with people who had a very similar profile to mine.

![Score progression on Mathraining]({{ "/_images/mathraining/score-graph.png" | relative_url }})
{: .img-wrap}

I then did a huge number of exercises over 2 months, then came prépa, and I had to focus mainly on coursework. But by the end of prépa, I obviously had a much better level, and with a bit of motivation, I quickly climbed to "Expert." Then a small pause once in engineering school, since I had other things to focus on again, and I got back to working seriously on the site during the summer break of 2025. That's when I reached "Master," the last rank before "Grandmaster," the highest one available.

The site's main topics are combinatorics, geometry, number theory, algebra, inequalities, and functional equations. I'm especially a fan of number theory, so I made sure to finish that category. In geometry, on the other hand, I'm still quite far off...

![Progress by topic on Mathraining]({{ "/_images/mathraining/chapitres.png" | relative_url }})
{: .img-wrap}

I've also been a grader for about a year now, meaning I help grade students' solutions. I clearly haven't graded as many solutions as the other graders, but I'm glad I can help reduce their workload from time to time.
